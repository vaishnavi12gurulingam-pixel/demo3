Booking questions

Before you enquire

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

How soon will I hear back?

Within 24 hours, on weekdays and weekends.

Can I change my date after enquiring?

Yes. Tell us the new date and we will check availability for you.

Do you need access to the venue early?

For full rigs we ask for early access to set up and sound-check before doors open.

What should I send with my enquiry?

The date, the hours, the guest count and the room. Photos or a floor plan help too.
